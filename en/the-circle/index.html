After paying, create your account on My account using the email you paid with. I open your access by hand — allow a few hours. After that the same email and password open the site, the forum and the Studio.

Yes, one account for everything: same email, same password. Change it once and it changes everywhere.

You lose access to the Studio, the forum and the Circle guides. Any guide you bought separately stays yours forever — the membership doesn't touch it.
